Offerings for elementary school visits

Programs below include content adjusted for grade level and run 20–30 minutes for younger grades up through 2nd, 35–45 minutes for grades 3 and up.

It's Story Time! (for Grades Pre K — 2nd)

  • An interactive read-aloud of Strolling Adventure and sing-a-long with live guitar
  • Learn colors and numbers
  • Discuss what we see on the page
  • About 20 minutes

Making a Picture Book (Grades 2–5)

  • Intro to the book, including the original idea
  • Reading of the book and discussion of nature illustrated
  • Creative illustration process step by step
  • Q&A

Virginia Natural Habitat (Grades 4–5)

  • Discuss some of the nature illustrated in the book
  • Identify ways we are connected to nature
  • Share ways items in the book are related to each other
  • Q&A

An idea is not just an idea (Grades 2–5)

  • Discuss kernels of an idea when starting the storytelling process
  • How might we illustrate
  • How does illustration contribute to the telling of a story
  • Q&A

Mindset of an illustrator (Grades K–5)

  • Who likes to draw?
  • Identifying audience
  • Sharing of the journey to define signature style
  • How it all comes together
  • Q&A
