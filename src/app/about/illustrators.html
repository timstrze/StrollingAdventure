Illustrators

Brandy Taylor Strzelecki

Brandy Taylor Strzelecki is an artist with over 20 years of professional experience in print and digital design. She enjoys the creative process of combining handmade watercolor patterns and whimsical illustrations to bring storytelling to life. She often finds herself marveling over the textures found in nature and then creating her own inspired patterns.

She loves art, interior design, and eating popsicles with her children on her back porch swing. Brandy lives with her husband and two children in historic Yorktown, Virginia.

Amber Marie Taylor

Amber Marie Taylor enjoys creating her own decorative, hand-written texts. She also creates her personal artwork using watercolors or colored pencils, with her favorite subjects being ocean or sea creatures. She also enjoys reading, painting, drawing, and experimenting with new culinary dishes.
